## Purpose

Reviews (1-5 estrelas) e recomendações por dia de jantar.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Add a review to a day
Any person SHALL be able to add a review entry to any day in the schedule. A review MUST include a star rating from 1 to 5 and MAY include free-text comment up to 500 characters. Reviews are anonymous — no author identity is stored.

#### Scenario: Submit a review with stars and text
- **WHEN** a person selects "Review", picks 4 stars, writes "Estava ótimo, repetia" and submits on day 2026-08-03
- **THEN** a new review entry is stored under that day with stars=4, text="Estava ótimo, repetia", and a timestamp, visible to everyone viewing the schedule

#### Scenario: Submit a review with stars only
- **WHEN** a person selects "Review", picks 5 stars, leaves the text empty and submits
- **THEN** a new review entry is stored with stars=5 and an empty text

#### Scenario: Reject a review without stars
- **WHEN** a person selects "Review" and tries to submit without picking a star rating
- **THEN** the submission is blocked client-side and the person is prompted to pick a rating first

### Requirement: Add a recommendation to a day
Any person SHALL be able to add a recommendation entry to any day in the schedule. A recommendation MUST include free-text (a dish suggestion) up to 500 characters and MUST NOT include a star rating. Recommendations are anonymous.

#### Scenario: Submit a recommendation
- **WHEN** a person selects "Recomendação", writes "Que tal um cozido à portuguesa?" and submits on day 2026-08-10
- **THEN** a new recommendation entry is stored under that day with the text and a timestamp, and no stars value

#### Scenario: Reject an empty recommendation
- **WHEN** a person selects "Recomendação" and tries to submit with empty text
- **THEN** the submission is blocked client-side

### Requirement: Display feedback entries per day
The system SHALL display all review and recommendation entries for a day within that day's card, ordered chronologically, showing the star rating (for reviews) and the text (when present).

#### Scenario: Multiple entries shown together
- **WHEN** a day has two reviews (4 and 5 stars) and one recommendation
- **THEN** all three entries appear listed in the day's card, in the order they were added, each visibly tagged as review (with stars) or recommendation

#### Scenario: Day with no feedback
- **WHEN** a day has no review or recommendation entries
- **THEN** the day's card shows the feedback section with an empty/neutral state and the add-entry form, without any entry listed

### Requirement: Real-time sync with local fallback
Feedback entries SHALL sync in real time across devices when Firebase is configured, using the same connectivity model as the existing menu sync (anonymous auth, same project). When Firebase is not configured or the connection fails, entries SHALL be persisted to `localStorage` on the current device only.

#### Scenario: Firebase configured and reachable
- **WHEN** two devices have the site open on the same day and one adds a review
- **THEN** the other device's view updates with the new review without a page reload

#### Scenario: Firebase not configured
- **WHEN** `assets/firebase-config.js` still has placeholder values
- **THEN** adding a review or recommendation stores it in `localStorage` and it is only visible on that device

### Requirement: Feedback summary in the copy-to-group text
The text generated by "Copiar escala para o grupo" SHALL include, per day, the average star rating (rounded to one decimal) when the day has at least one review, and the count of recommendations when the day has at least one, without listing individual comments.

#### Scenario: Day with reviews and recommendations
- **WHEN** a day has reviews of 4 and 5 stars, and 2 recommendations
- **THEN** the copied text for that day includes an average of 4.5 stars and a note that there are 2 recommendations

#### Scenario: Day with no feedback
- **WHEN** a day has no review or recommendation entries
- **THEN** the copied text for that day omits the feedback summary line entirely
